# Research basis (September 2026)

The research that defined the curriculum. The topic map covers three layers: the **industry** in general, **Foxway** as a company, and the **Benelux** region. A commercial layer on top turns the knowledge into selling skills.

## Known limitations
- Public-web research only; foxway.com pages could not be read directly from the build environment, so Foxway facts rely on press releases, reports and reputable coverage.
- No internal Foxway Benelux data (customers, targets, organisation). Add it through Team Knowledge packs.
- Some survey figures (e.g. Belgian adoption) come from older consumer studies and are labelled as such in the lessons.
